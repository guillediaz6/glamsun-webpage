✦ Born in Gran Canaria · Based in Switzerland (Zürich) · DJ · Producer · Singer ✦

Agatha Sun

Music has been part of my DNA since I was 4 years old, growing up in a family of musicians and guided by my father. As a vinyl DJ, my artistic journey began with dance, singing, and performance, taking me across stages, music videos, television, and collaborations with Canarian artists and Los 40 Principales.

I have lived in five countries and experienced over 50 cities—journeys that have deeply enriched my artistic and musical identity.

Currently, I am developing my career as a DJ and producer, delivering an energetic and intense sound influenced by Tech House, Progressive, and Underground. My initial releases, “ROTATION” and “SYNTHEC”, mark this new chapter, alongside the upcoming release “FLAYING”.

✦ RESIDENT RADIO STATIONS ✦

I am a resident on PURE IBIZA RADIO on Mondays at 22:00 (Ibiza local time), on ACTIVA HITS on Saturdays at 22:00 (local time), and on RADIO TOP 21 on Fridays from 20:00 to 22:00 (live show).

I have brought my music to Ibiza, Gran Canaria, Zürich, and London, and I am the founder of my own brand, GLAM SUN.
